01 / The journey
The idea

Less sightseeing.
More being somewhere.

This trip is built around the places we sleep, not a checklist of attractions. Five nights in Kiso Valley and four nights in Yamanaka Onsen give us time to settle in, soak, read, explore slowly and take a walk when we feel like it. Kyoto and Tokyo add good food, galleries and a change of pace.

What we're choosing

Forest views over big hikes. Traditional rooms over generic hotels. Onsen evenings over early starts. Train travel over hiring a car. A real holiday, not an endurance event.

The heart of the holiday

Two ryokans, chosen.

These are the two accommodation choices we're keeping. Both have returned availability for the full stay on Booking.com, but neither is reserved.

ON-YADO TSUTAYA · KISO-FUKUSHIMA
Stay 01 · 22–27 November · 5 nights

Onyado
Tsutaya

A centuries-old inn in a historic stretch of the Kiso Valley. Tatami rooms, the Kiso River, hot baths and the sense of being in a real Japanese town rather than a resort precinct.

Traditional ryokanOnsenNakasendō countrysideRail accessible

Important: book a Japanese-style room in the main building, not the separate Western-style Hatago annex. Confirm dinner/breakfast for the selected rate and arrival cut-off after the flight.

The most atmospheric place on the itinerary: a classic ryokan above Kakusenkei Gorge, with forest views, an open-air onsen and a beautiful riverside path just outside.

Before paying for anything

Three things to confirm.

01 / 🛏

The rooms

For Tsutaya, choose the Japanese-style main building, not the Western-style annex. For Tawaraya, check that the room overlooks the forested gorge if that view matters to us.

Tsutaya's official room guide ↗
02 / 🍱

The meals

Confirm which nights include dinner and breakfast, not just whether the inn has a restaurant. Four nights of kaiseki may be lovely—or more food than we really want.

03 / 🚆

The connections

Verify Tsutaya's latest dinner arrival time and reserve Tawaraya's complimentary Kaga Onsen Station shuttle. Tawaraya currently publishes pickups at 14:00–18:00.
